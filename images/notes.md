# Bridging Web Accessibility Gaps

- **Current Challenges:** The UCC website currently has partially non-compliant content under WCAG 2.1 Level AA, including images lacking alt text, uncaptioned videos, and inaccessible downloadable documents.
- **AI Solutions:** AI-powered tools make inclusive design more efficient by automating the generation of image alt text, providing speech-to-text video transcripts, and performing content readability checks.
- **Multilingual Support:** AI can also facilitate language translation to break down barriers for international students. 

<!--
Narrator's Notes:

# Optimizing for AI Search Engines (AIO)

- **Technical SEO Foundation:** AI crawlers reward flawless technical setups. Utilizing specific Schema Markup (e.g., *Organization*, *Course*, *Event*) tells AI exactly what UCC's content is about.
- **Core Web Vitals:** AI algorithms heavily prioritize the user experience, demanding optimal site speed (Largest Contentful Paint), responsiveness (Interaction to Next Paint), and visual stability (Cumulative Layout Shift).

<!--
Narrator's Notes: 
As Google and other search engines evolve into AI-driven answering systems, we must adapt to AIO—AI Search Engine Optimization. AI algorithms love structure. By implementing precise Schema Markup on our program and event pages, we feed the AI exactly what it needs to rank our courses highly. Furthermore, AI heavily rewards websites that offer a frictionless user experience. We must ensure our Core Web Vitals—like loading speed and visual stability—are pristine to remain visible in this new era of search.

# The Necessity of Human Oversight

- **Ethical & Authentic Engagement:** While AI improves efficiency, it cannot replace the authenticity of a real student voice. Trust drives final enrollment decisions.
- **Quality Control:** AI is prone to errors in translation, image recognition, and cultural nuances. Human review remains a strict requirement before publishing AI-generated content.
- **Superficiality:** AI can generate content of a reasonable standard, but it often lacks real depth and critical engagement with the subject matter.

<!--
